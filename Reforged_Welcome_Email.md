Hi {{first_name}},

Welcome to Reforged — I'm really glad you're here.

I built Reforged because I wanted a Bible app that helped Scripture actually *stick*: a calm, ad-free space to read the Word, memorize it, and carry it with you through the day. No noise, no paywalls. Just you and God's Word.

Here's the best way to start:

1. **Read a chapter today.** Pick any translation — ESV, KJV, CSB, NKJV, or NASB — and begin your streak. Even five minutes counts.
2. **Memorize your first verse.** Try something short like Philippians 4:13. Reforged uses spaced repetition, so verses actually move into long-term memory instead of fading by tomorrow.
3. **Turn on the daily reminder.** A gentle nudge is the difference between wanting to build a habit and actually building one.

That's really it. Read it. Memorize it. Live it.

One honest note: Reforged is free, ad-free, and made by one person — and it stays that way because readers choose to support it. There's no pressure at all today; I just wanted you to know that if it ever becomes meaningful to you, there's a way to help keep it free for everyone. For now, simply enjoy it.

If you ever have a question, a favorite verse, or an idea to make Reforged better, just reply to this email — it comes straight to me, and I read every one.

Grace and peace,
The maker of Reforged
