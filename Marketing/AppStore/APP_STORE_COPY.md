## Description (4000 max)

Most Bible apps help you read a verse. Reforged helps you keep it.

Reforged turns reading, study, and Scripture memory into a daily habit that sticks. Five calm minutes a day is enough to start. It is free, ad-free, and built to respect your privacy.

Read it. Memorize it. Live it.

MEMORIZE SCRIPTURE THAT STAYS WITH YOU
• Follow a guided Memory Journey through 150 verses in 15 themed units, one step at a time
• Spaced repetition brings each verse back right before you would forget it
• Six practice modes grow with you: flashcards, tap to reveal, drag and drop, fill in the blank, first letter, and type it out
• Say It Back: recite out loud and watch each word light up as you get it right
• Quick daily lessons, matching games, and complete-the-verse challenges
• Build your own collections, or bring saved verses over from another Bible app

READ THE WHOLE BIBLE, YOUR WAY
• KJV, ESV, NLT, NKJV, CSB, NASB, NET, and the Spanish Reina-Valera 1960
• Greek and Hebrew texts (TR, SBLGNT, WLC) for deeper study
• Listen to narrated ESV and KJV audio, with KJV available offline
• Highlights, notes, search, and beautiful verse cards to share
• Reading plans, including Spurgeon's Morning and Evening, or build your own
• One Continue button always takes you to your next reading
• Adjust fonts, size, and spacing, including a dyslexia-friendly font

STUDY DEEPER
• Tap any word for its original Hebrew or Greek meaning, built in and available offline
• Classic commentaries, Bible dictionaries, and topical guides
• A Bible atlas that maps the places you read about
• Guided learning tracks with passages, quizzes, and reflection

WALK WITH FRIENDS
• Add friends with an invite code, a QR scan, or your contacts
• Memorize a verse together and build a shared streak
• Read a plan or passage together and see when your friend has read today
• Send a friend a verse challenge or a quick quiz
• Encouraging nudges only. No chat feed, no follower counts

BUILD A DAILY RHYTHM
• A guided Morning Routine: Scripture, prayer, gratitude, and review
• My Disciplines: create your own daily checklist of habits to keep and things to avoid, with reminders you choose
• Notes and journal for reflections, prayers, and Bible notes, organized in folders
• Gentle reminders that stay off until you turn them on

GUARD YOUR HEART AND FOCUS
• Focus & Purity Shield uses Screen Time to block harmful content and distracting apps
• Unlock social apps each day by spending time in the Word first
• Invite a trusted partner to hold your protection with a private PIN
• "I'm Being Tempted" walks you through Scripture, prayer, and one next step, right when you need it, with grace if you have stumbled

STAY ENCOURAGED
• Earn XP, level up, and keep your streak alive, with streak freezes for hard days
• Earn crowns for real progress and spend them on themes, verse card styles, avatar frames, and reading extras. Crowns are earned, never sold
• Daily insights and verse of the day
• Home screen widgets for your verse of the day, your streak, and a quick way to get help when tempted

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
A full iPad experience with a sidebar and study tools beside your reading. Your progress, verses, notes, and preferences sync privately through iCloud, so a new device feels like home.

FOR EVERY CHRISTIAN
Reforged is non-denominational and welcomes believers from every tradition. Classic study resources are offered as tools for learning, not as a doctrinal stance.

Also includes the Walk Talks podcast, made in partnership with Southland Christian Camp, and the Southland 2026 camp verses as their own memory track.

FREE AND READER-SUPPORTED
No ads and no subscriptions. If Reforged helps you, an optional tip keeps it growing.

Optional AI features help with insights, search, and reflection prompts. Always test what you read against Scripture.

Walk With Friends
Scripture sticks when you share it. Add friends with an invite code, a QR scan, or your contacts. Memorize a verse together, take quizzes, build a shared streak, read a plan side by side, and send a verse challenge that opens straight to the verse.

Also in this update:

• A new device feels like home: your reading and display choices follow you
• A fuller SOS for temptation, including a path to confession and grace
